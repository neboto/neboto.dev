## A heading

Posts are ordinary Markdown, so everything the guide uses works here:
<kbd>/</kbd> keys, `inline code`, tables and fenced blocks.

```sh
neboto ls ec2 --region ap-southeast-2
```

> Blockquotes keep the guide's callout style.

To add a post, create `content/blog/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md` with a `title` and
`description`. The date comes from the filename.
